    What is a nutritional score?

    Nutri-Score is a label that rates the nutritional quality of a packaged food from A (best) to E. It adds up points for things to limit (energy, sugars, saturated fat, salt) and subtracts points for things to favour (fibre, protein, fruit, vegetables and legumes). The lower the final score, the better the grade.
